# 🤖 Smart Resume Genie

> **AI-powered ATS Resume Analyzer** designed to help job seekers evaluate, improve, and optimize their resumes for modern Applicant Tracking Systems.

🌐 **Live Application:** https://smartresumegenie.vercel.app/

---

## 📌 Overview

**Smart Resume Genie** is a web-based resume analysis platform designed to help candidates understand how well their resume aligns with modern recruitment and **Applicant Tracking System (ATS)** requirements.

The platform focuses on providing actionable resume feedback and **HR-oriented insights** so users can identify potential weaknesses and improve the overall quality, relevance, and ATS compatibility of their resume.

Instead of manually reviewing every aspect of a resume, users can use Smart Resume Genie as a digital resume-review assistant.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Modern companies often use **Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS)** to process large numbers of resumes before they reach recruiters.

A resume may contain strong qualifications but still perform poorly if it:

* Uses difficult-to-parse formatting
* Lacks relevant keywords
* Does not clearly communicate skills
* Has weak job-description alignment
* Contains unnecessary information
* Does not follow common resume conventions

This creates a problem for candidates who may not know why their resume is being overlooked.

### 💡 Proposed Solution

Smart Resume Genie provides an automated resume-analysis workflow that helps candidates identify areas that can be improved before submitting their applications.

The goal is to make professional resume optimization more accessible to students, fresh graduates, and job seekers.

## 🔐 Privacy & Security

Resume documents may contain sensitive personal information such as:

* Name
* Email address
* Phone number
* Education
* Employment history
* Skills
* Project information

Therefore, production implementations should consider:

* Secure file processing
* Data minimization
* Secure API communication
* Protection of uploaded resumes
* Secure environment variables
* Prevention of unauthorized access
* Appropriate deletion/retention policies

Users should avoid uploading confidential documents unless they understand how the application processes and stores them.

## ⚠️ Important Disclaimer

Smart Resume Genie provides automated resume-analysis guidance and should be treated as a **career-support tool**, not as a guarantee of ATS performance, interview selection, or employment.

Different organizations use different recruitment systems and screening processes. A resume score or recommendation should therefore be interpreted as guidance rather than an official hiring assessment.
